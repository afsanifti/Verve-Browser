# Verve Browser — Release Notes (November 18, 2026)

## What's New

### 1. Chrome-Style 1:1 Swipe-to-Switch Tabs
- **Finger-Tracking Carousel:** Swiping left or right on the bottom search pill moves the active webpage 1:1 with your thumb while pulling the adjacent tab's preview in across a crisp 12dp dark gutter.
- **Expanded & Collapsed Support:** Works seamlessly in both the full toolbar and the collapsed mini-pill state.
- **Themed Fallback Cards:** Displays site titles and icons for New Tabs, Incognito tabs, or pages without a captured snapshot yet.

### 2. 2-Column Grid View in Quick Tabs
- **Layout Switcher:** Added a top-bar toggle in Quick Tabs to switch between the stacked card carousel and a Chrome-style 2-column grid.
- **Refined Grid Cards:** Concentric rounded cards feature site monogram/icon badges, tab stack labels, one-tap close buttons, and primary highlighting for the active tab.
- **Smooth Navigation:** Auto-scrolls to keep the active tab visible and animates cleanly between grid and carousel views.

## Improvements & Polish

### 3. Bottom Bar on Non-Scrollable Pages
- **Smart Accessibility:** Keeps the floating bottom toolbar accessible on short or non-scrollable pages so it never gets stuck collapsed.
- **Manual Gestures Preserved:** You can still swipe vertically on the pill to manually collapse or expand it on demand.

### 4. Smoother, Bounce-Free Motion
- **Critically Damped Curves:** Replaced rubbery springs with smooth, zero-overshoot motion across the Quick Tabs stack, bottom pill transitions, and lateral tab carousel.
- **Refined Fling Physics:** Tuned horizontal velocity handoffs in Quick Tabs so cards glide and settle cleanly into focus.

### 5. Zero-Stutter Tab Switching
- **Snapshot-First Transitions:** Animates cached tab snapshots throughout bottom-bar drags and release glides, swapping in the live `WebView` only after settling to eliminate frame drops.
