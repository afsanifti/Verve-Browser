✨ What's New

- **Reader Mode**: Distill articles into a clean, distraction-free reading layout styled with your Material 3 theme colors, complete with a quick-exit indicator in the search pill and system Back button support
- **Built-In Page Translation & Language Preferences**:
- Translate webpages in place via the Page Actions sheet with a full-height language picker, live source → target status (`English ↔ Spanish`), and instant restoration to the original page
- Configure default target language, drag-to-reorder preferred website languages (`Accept-Language`), auto-translate rules, and "never translate" exclusions in **Settings → Languages**
- Automatic prompt banner when visiting pages in foreign languages, with quick **Translate**, **Always translate**, and **Never translate** actions


- **3-State Verve Shields**: Tap the shield quick toggle to cycle between **Off**, **Standard**, and **Aggressive** modes, while automatically defaulting to your Settings preference on new pages
- **Smoother Toolbar Animations**: Eliminated layout jitter when collapsing and expanding the bottom toolbar, and centered the search pill on the New Tab screen
- **Smarter Download Filtering**: Prevented silent background API and telemetry responses (such as `.json` and `sgasync`) from triggering unwanted automatic downloads
- Stability and improvement on adblocker (including CNAME uncloaking startup fixes)
