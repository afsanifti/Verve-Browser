### Significant Changes

- **Find in Page:** Search for text on any webpage right from the bottom bar, complete with a live match counter and quick up/down navigation.
- **Downloads Manager:** View, open, and share your files in a new Downloads screen, with a live progress banner at the top whenever a download starts.
- **Faster Browsing with HTTP/3:** Added QUIC / HTTP/3 support for quicker page loads, along with a toggle in Settings.
- **Simpler App Updates:** Tapping **Update** now takes you straight to **About Verve** and automatically starts downloading and installing the update.

### Improvements & Stability

- **Bottom Bar & Gestures:** Made the bottom bar animations smoother and refined tab-swiping previews, text zoom controls, and desktop site mode.
- **Cleaner Icons & Menus:** Updated the site controls icon and polished the page actions menu.
- **Bug Fixes & Reliability:** Fixed an issue where downloaded APKs would re-download instead of installing, improved back-button navigation, and added automatic cleanup for old update files.
