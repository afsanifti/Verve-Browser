✨ **What's New**

* **Configurable Default Search Engine**: Choose from 10 popular search engines—including Google, Microsoft Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Startpage, Ecosia, Qwant, Yahoo!, Yandex, and Kagi—with dedicated brand icons and matching search suggestions in **Settings → Search Engine**
* **Search-Engine-Independent Incognito Mode**: Isolated cookie, storage, and profile sessions prevent regular-account logins from leaking to any search engine, while disabling local history, keyboard learning, and remote autocomplete suggestions until the last Incognito tab closes
* **Persistent Download History**: Downloaded files now stay saved in the **Downloads** list across app restarts, with interrupted downloads automatically preserved so they can be resumed
* **Reader Mode**: Clean, distraction-free article view styled with your Material 3 theme colors and a quick-exit badge in the search pill
* **Built-In Page Translation & Language Preferences**: In-place webpage translation, automatic language detection prompts, and customizable preferred/auto-translate rules in **Settings → Languages**
* **3-State Verve Shields**: Quick-cycle between **Off**, **Standard**, and **Aggressive** protection modes
* **Performance & UI Polish**: Smoother jitter-free toolbar animations, centered New Tab search pill, smarter background download filtering, and adblocker stability fixes
